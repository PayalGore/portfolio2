About

The Story Behind
the Data

I'm Payal — a data analyst who thinks in pipelines and speaks in dashboards. I bridge the gap between raw, chaotic data and the clear, confident decisions that move organizations forward.

At DocGo, I automated AR reconciliation and ASC 606 revenue recognition with SQL/dbt pipelines, cutting manual reporting effort by 40%. At IU Bloomington, I run nutrition study analytics and CRM segmentation that boosted campaign participation by 25%. I don't just build dashboards — I build the semantic layers and data models that make them trustworthy at scale.

Before grad school, I ran a produce retail & wholesale venture in Mumbai — analyzing demand patterns, supplier costs, and spoilage data across 15+ categories. That hands-on business ownership shaped how I think about data: not as an academic exercise, but as a survival tool for real decisions.
